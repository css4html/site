An **algorithm** is a sequence of steps that solves a problem. A recipe is an everyday example: order matters, and each instruction must be clear enough to follow without guessing.

## Input → processing → output

To calculate change when the payment covers the price:

1. **Input:** receive the amount paid and the price.
2. **Processing:** subtract the price from the amount paid.
3. **Output:** show the change.

Write the steps in your own words before typing. Reversing the subtraction changes the answer.

<aside class="tip tip-logic"><strong>LOGIC TIP</strong> — Understand the problem first; then turn each step into code.</aside>

## Try the console
