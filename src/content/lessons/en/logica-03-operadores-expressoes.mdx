An **expression** combines values and operators to produce a result. `3 * 10` gives `30`; `30 >= 18` gives `true`.

| Operator | Purpose | Example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `+`, `-` | Add and subtract | `10 - 3` → `7` |
| `*`, `/` | Multiply and divide | `3 * 10` → `30` |
| `%` | Division remainder | `7 % 2` → `1` |
| `===`, `!==` | Strict equality and inequality | `20 === '20'` → `false` |
| `>`, `<`, `>=`, `<=` | Compare values | `18 >= 18` → `true` |

Prefer `===` to `==` as you start: strict comparison does not automatically convert text to numbers. With text, `+` may concatenate: `'2' + 3` gives `'23'`.

## Combine conditions

For booleans, `&&` means **AND**: both must be true. `||` means **OR**: at least one must be true. A fictional party rule is: at least 18 years old AND (has an invitation OR is accompanied).

Write `idade >= 18 && (temConvite || acompanhado)`. Parentheses make the rule clear. `>` excludes 18; `>=` includes it.

<aside class="tip tip-logic"><strong>LOGIC TIP</strong> — State the rule in words before combining operators.</aside>
